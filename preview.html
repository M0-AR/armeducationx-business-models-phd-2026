🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are a professional

Let's work this out in a step-by-step way to be sure we have the right answer. No prior chip knowledge needed. Each row takes ~2 minutes.

Step 1 — What is a business model?
How a technology earns money and keeps earning it. Example: sell blueprints once + tiny fee per product forever. That “forever” part is the annuity you measure in Experiment 1.
Step 2 — Digitize vs digitalize vs transform?
Scan paper → digitize. Change the process (subscriptions) → digitalize. Change who pays whom in the ecosystem → transform. Experiment 2 gives you 18 examples and a checker that scores 100%.
Step 3 — Why partners matter more than factories?
1000+ companies + 22M developers building on one blueprint = leaving costs more than staying. Experiment 3 draws this network and proves removing the hub breaks 64.7% of it.
Step 4 — How do trends prove it?
New server blueprints ship 2.22× faster than history; research spending rises every year. Experiment 4 fetches live stocks, currencies and R&D to confirm.
Step 5 — Which model fits YOUR idea?
Answer 6 questions (student? startup? volume? need full system or just chip?). Experiment 5 picks: Academic / Flexible / Total Access / Full System / Finished Chip — and tells you when to pivot next.
Step 6 — What hidden pattern makes you look smart in interviews?
Say: “Licensing leads royalties by 2–4 years, so today’s licenses predict 2028 revenue; and each stack step roughly doubles the rate.” Both are measured here.
